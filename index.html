What it does

Clean ClickGUI

One panel per category, small flat module buttons, sliders and keybinds. Drag panels where you want them, your layout is saved.

Inventory menu

Open your inventory and get a compact menu next to it: delay and send packets, view the queue, log traffic and run macros.

Adjustable hitboxes

Make other players' hitboxes bigger in every direction, so you can hit them without aiming exactly. You set how much extra reach you want.

Server-friendly mode

Switch between a safe mode that stays inside the vanilla and Paper movement limits, and an unrestricted mode for servers with no rules.

Download

You need Fabric Loader and Fabric API for Minecraft 1.21.11. Put the INDX .jar into your mods folder and start the game. Open the ClickGUI with Right Shift.
